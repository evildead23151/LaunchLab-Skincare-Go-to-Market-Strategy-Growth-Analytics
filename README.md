## Executive summary

The project investigates whether first-time skincare buyers need help translating ingredient and product information into a clear, trustworthy first-purchase decision. Ingredient disclosure alone may not resolve questions about product purpose, routine order, realistic expectations, price, evidence and product choice.

The proposed campaign platform, **The Ingredient Clarity Edit**, would use accessible, evidence-linked educational content to help beginners understand publicly available product information and navigate to relevant existing brand resources. This is a hypothesis to test—not a validated customer insight or a claim that Minimalist lacks education tools.

Minimalist already communicates ingredient transparency and offers Skin Insights and curated kits. Competitors also communicate ingredient education, science-led positioning or skin assessment. The case study therefore focuses on identifying a potentially useful communication journey, not claiming that transparency itself is unique.

## Project objective

Produce a defensible product-marketing recommendation supported by dated public research, primary consumer research, audience segmentation, evidence-backed positioning, messaging, channel and launch strategy, campaign assets and—if feasible—a measurable landing-page/content experiment.

## Initial audience hypothesis

College students and first-time skincare buyers aged 18–24 in India who are beginning to build a basic routine and value clear, understandable product information.

India-wide digital context may be researched, but a convenience sample recruited through campuses or social networks is not nationally representative.

## Core research questions

1. What makes a first-time skincare buyer trust or distrust a brand?
2. Do ingredient names and concentrations help, confuse or overwhelm beginners?
3. Which information matters before purchase: product purpose, ingredient explanation, routine order, evidence, price, reviews or expert input?
4. How do consumers discover and evaluate skincare products?
5. What role do creators, friends, dermatologists, marketplaces and brand websites play?
6. How do beginners experience Minimalist's existing product pages, Skin Insights and kits?
7. Which objections prevent trial or purchase?
