## Recent behaviour

1. Tell me about the last skincare product you purchased.
2. How did you discover it?
3. What information did you look for before purchasing?
4. What made you trust or distrust the brand/product?
5. Did you understand the ingredients and product claims?
6. What was confusing or difficult?
7. What alternatives did you consider?
8. What ultimately drove the purchase decision?
