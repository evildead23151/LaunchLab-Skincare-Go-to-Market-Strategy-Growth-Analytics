# Consumer Survey — Draft

**Target:** Adults aged 18–24 in India who are students or early-career consumers.  
**Purpose:** Exploratory research on skincare discovery, ingredient comprehension and purchase confidence.  
**Status:** Draft. Pilot for clarity and consent before distribution.

## Participant information and consent

This independent student project explores how young adults discover and evaluate skincare products. Participation is voluntary. Do not provide medical information, diagnoses, photographs or contact details. Responses will be analysed in aggregate for a portfolio case study. This project is not affiliated with any skincare brand and does not provide medical advice.

Confirm participant is 18–24 before proceeding. Do not collect responses from minors.

## Draft questions

1. Are you aged 18–24? (Yes / No; end survey if No.)
2. Are you currently a student or early-career professional?
3. How would you describe your skincare experience? (First-time / Beginner / Familiar / Experienced)
4. Where do you usually discover skincare products? (Brand websites / Instagram / YouTube / Friends or family / Dermatologist / Marketplace / Retail / Other)
5. What information do you check before buying? (Ingredients / Concentrations / Product purpose / Reviews / Price / Brand reputation / Expert advice / Other)
6. How easy is it to understand ingredient names and concentrations? (1–5 scale with labelled endpoints)
7. Which part is most confusing? (Ingredient purpose / Product selection / Routine order / Product combinations / Claims / Price / Authenticity / None / Other)
8. What makes a skincare brand trustworthy? (Select up to three; include Other)
9. Have you abandoned a skincare purchase because you were unsure which product to choose? (Yes / No / Prefer not to say)
10. What did you do instead? (Optional open text)
11. Which format would help you understand a product? (Short explainer / Ingredient glossary / Routine guide / Comparison table / Expert-led content / Reviews / Other)
12. What price range feels acceptable for a basic product? (Set ranges only after pilot testing; include Prefer not to say)
13. Have you heard of Minimalist? (Yes / No / Not sure)
14. If yes, what comes to mind? (Optional open text)
15. What would make skincare product information clearer? (Optional open text)

## Analysis cautions

- Pilot with 3–5 people for clarity.
- A convenience sample is not representative of all Indian 18–24-year-olds.
- Report denominators with every percentage.
- Do not imply causality from descriptive survey data.
- Avoid unnecessary health or sensitive personal data.
