# Assets

Store original project-created assets here: campaign mockups, original diagrams, landing-page screenshots and presentation visuals.

Before adding an asset:
- identify its creator and source;
- record whether it is original, licensed, or used under a permitted basis;
- do not copy competitor campaign creatives or logos into portfolio deliverables without checking reuse rights;
- label all mockups as concepts and avoid implying brand approval;
- do not upload participant photographs, identifiable information or private company material.
