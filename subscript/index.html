ABOUT THIS EXPERIENCE

Built to explore business decisions.

Subscript is an independent portfolio dashboard for subscription revenue, customer retention, unit economics and pricing scenarios. All customers, transactions and business metrics are synthetic demonstration data.

The pricing lab recalculates a scenario using assumptions selected by the visitor. It does not predict real customer behaviour or represent a validated forecast.
