# Brand and Campaign Context

## Brand studied

Minimalist (Uprising Science) is the focal existing brand. The project studies its publicly available brand positioning, product communication and customer-facing resources. It does not represent the brand or access internal data.

## Audience

College students and first-time skincare buyers aged 18–24 in India. This is an audience hypothesis for research, not a proven segment definition.

## Working campaign platform

**The Ingredient Clarity Edit** — a proposed educational campaign that helps beginners understand:
- what an ingredient is described as doing in a product;
- why a product is positioned for a particular use;
- how the brand describes routine order and product combinations;
- where to find supporting information and limitations;
- which questions should be referred to a qualified professional rather than answered by marketing content.

## Strategic boundary

Minimalist already communicates ingredient transparency and has existing Skin Insights and curated-kit resources. Therefore, the proposed campaign must not claim to invent transparency, personalised routine guidance or kits. It should investigate whether the information is sufficiently understandable and find an incremental education or acquisition opportunity.

## Safety and claims discipline

- Do not diagnose skin conditions or offer medical advice.
- Do not promise clinical outcomes.
- Do not imply a product is suitable for every person.
- Do not recommend combining actives without relying on approved brand guidance and appropriate expert review.
- Do not convert a marketing claim into an independently verified efficacy finding.
- Link claims to the brand's current public product information and record access dates.

## Decisions still open

- Which beginner question is most important?
- Does the audience prefer ingredient-led, concern-led or routine-led education?
- Which content format improves understanding?
- Which existing brand resource should the campaign connect to?
- Is there a viable acquisition channel beyond existing brand activity?
