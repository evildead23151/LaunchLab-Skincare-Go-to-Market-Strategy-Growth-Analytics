# Phase 1 — Competitor Benchmark Protocol

## Objective

Understand how the focal brand and four comparison brands communicate ingredient information, trust, beginner education, product selection, pricing and purchase guidance.

## Brands

1. Minimalist — focal brand.
2. The Derma Co — direct category competitor.
3. Foxtale — direct category competitor.
4. Plum Goodness — adjacent competitor.
5. Dot & Key — adjacent competitor.

This is an initial comparison set, not an exhaustive market map.

## Method

This phase is desk research based on public pages and observable customer journeys. It is not a hands-on review of internal tools or a claim about actual business performance.

For every observation record:
- brand and competitor type;
- exact source URL;
- page or product name;
- access date;
- observation (what is visibly stated or shown);
- interpretation (what it may imply);
- confidence;
- limitation or question requiring validation.

## Benchmark dimensions

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Positioning | What does the brand claim to stand for? |
| Audience | Which buyer is explicitly addressed? |
| Ingredient transparency | Are ingredients, concentrations or formulation explanations shown? |
| Trust signals | What evidence, expert credentials, testing or reviews are presented? |
| Beginner education | Are terms explained in accessible language? |
| Routine guidance | Are steps, sequencing or kits provided? |
| Product architecture | How are concerns, ingredients and products organised? |
| Pricing | What are MRP, selling price, size and unit price? |
| Offers | Which bundles, coupons or promotions appear? |
| Channels | Which public D2C, marketplace, retail or social channels are visible? |
| Journey | What steps separate discovery from product selection? |
| Post-purchase | What education or support is publicly visible? |

## Price-capture protocol

For comparable products only, capture product name, category, size, MRP, visible selling price, discount, coupon condition, unit price, URL and access date. Do not compare unlike pack sizes without normalising. Dynamic prices are snapshots, not permanent pricing.

## Scoring rubric

Score each dimension 0–3 only after evidence capture:
- 0 — Not found in reviewed material (does not prove absence).
- 1 — Basic mention or limited explanation.
- 2 — Developed content or clear journey.
- 3 — Strong or integrated public experience.

Record confidence (High / Medium / Low) and the source for each score. Do not sum scores into an overall winner unless weighting is justified.

## Expected Phase 1 outputs

- Completed benchmark CSV.
- A 2–3 page competitor synthesis.
- A positioning map with explicit, evidence-based axes.
- Five category conventions / parity features.
- Three differentiation hypotheses to test with consumers.
- Updated source register.

## Interpretation discipline

Use "not found in reviewed public material," not "the competitor does not offer this." Marketing pages are incomplete representations of a product. Never infer efficacy, market share, customer satisfaction or conversion from product-page copy alone.
